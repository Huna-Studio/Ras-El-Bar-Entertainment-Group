Our Story

Creating Memories That Last a Lifetime

Nestled where the Nile kisses the Mediterranean, Ras El Bar Entertainment has been the heartbeat of summer in Egypt for over fifteen years. What began as a single amusement park has grown into a premier entertainment destination, home to two of the most beloved family attractions on the Mediterranean coast.

From the adrenaline-pumping thrills of Carnaval to the tranquil waters of Amwag, we craft experiences that bring families together, spark joy in children, and create stories worth telling for generations.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Carnaval is open daily from 10:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight). Amwag Resort operates from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. During peak summer season and special events, hours may be extended. Night events at Carnaval run until 2:00 AM on weekends.

Yes! Both destinations offer extensive parking facilities. Standard parking is free for all visitors. VIP parking is available near the main entrance for EGP 50. We also provide shuttle service from the Ras El Bar city center every 30 minutes.

Online ticket purchasing will be available soon through our website and mobile app. Currently, tickets can be purchased at the entrance gates. We recommend arriving early on weekends to avoid queues. Group bookings can be arranged in advance via WhatsApp.

Yes, safety is our priority. Thrill rides require a minimum height of 1.4m. Family rides are suitable for children 1.0m and above with adult supervision. Kids' attractions have no height restrictions. Height measurement stations are available at each ride entrance.

Outside food and beverages are not permitted, but we offer a wide variety of dining options across both destinations. From quick snacks to full-service restaurants, there's something for every taste and budget. Vegetarian and halal options are available at all dining outlets.

Absolutely! Groups of 15+ receive 15% off standard admission. School groups enjoy special educational rates with dedicated guides. Corporate events and birthday parties have custom packages. Contact us via WhatsApp for group booking arrangements.

For Carnaval: comfortable clothing, closed-toe shoes for thrill rides, sunscreen, and a hat. For Amwag: swimwear, towels, sunscreen, and water shoes. Lockers are available at both destinations. We provide life jackets for all water activities at no extra charge.

Yes, both destinations are fully wheelchair accessible with ramps, wide pathways, and accessible restrooms. Wheelchairs are available for rent at the entrance. Several rides and pools are adapted for guests with mobility needs. Our staff is trained to assist all visitors.
